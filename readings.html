Books and articles worth revisiting.

  • The 5 AM Club Robin Sharma · 2018 Self-help 4/5

    A morning routine framed as a story — waking early to protect time for exercise, reflection, and deep work before the day takes over.

  • The Metamorphosis Franz Kafka · 1915 Fiction 4.5/5

    Gregor Samsa wakes as an insect and becomes a stranger in his own home — a bleak, absurd meditation on alienation, guilt, and the cruelty of ordinary life.

  • The Lean Startup Eric Ries · 2011 Business 3.8/5

    How to build companies and products through continuous experimentation — validating ideas with customers, measuring learning, and pivoting before resources run out.

  • The 4-Hour Workweek Timothy Ferriss · 2007 Business 4/5

    A playbook for escaping the deferred-life plan — outsourcing, automation, and designing work around time and mobility instead of the other way around.

  • Atomic Habits James Clear · 2018 Self-help 3.8/5

    A practical framework for building good habits and breaking bad ones — focused on small, repeatable changes and the systems that make them stick.

  • The Art of War Sun Tzu · c. 5th century BC Philosophy 4/5

    A classic treatise on strategy, deception, and knowing when to fight — lessons that extend far beyond the battlefield into politics, business, and everyday conflict.

  • The Alchemist Paulo Coelho · 1988 Fiction 3.5/5

    A shepherd's journey in search of treasure becomes a fable about following your dreams, reading life's signs, and listening to your heart.

  • Your Erroneous Zones Wayne W. Dyer · 1976 Self-help 3.5/5

    A guide to spotting self-defeating habits — guilt, approval-seeking, worry, anger — and taking responsibility for the thoughts and choices that shape your emotional life.

  • The Twelve Apostles of the Peruvian Economy Francisco Durand · 2017 Economics 4/5

    An analysis of the twelve most influential economic power groups in Peru — six from Lima and six from the provinces — and how they convert wealth into political and social influence.
